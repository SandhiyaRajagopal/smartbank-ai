# SmartBank Schedule of Fees and Charges

*Fictional document created for the SmartBank AI demo. Effective 1 July 2026. All charges are exclusive of 18% GST.*

## Savings account
- **Minimum average monthly balance (AMB)**: Rs 10,000 in metro and urban branches; Rs 5,000 in semi-urban; Rs 2,500 in rural branches. Student accounts (age 18-25 with a valid student ID) have zero balance requirement.
- **Non-maintenance charge**: 6% of the shortfall in AMB, capped at Rs 500 per month. Charges are never applied if they would make the balance negative.
- **Cheque book**: first 25 leaves free per year; thereafter Rs 4 per leaf.
- **Account closure**: free within 14 days of opening or after 12 months; Rs 500 between 15 days and 12 months.

## Current account
- Minimum AMB of Rs 25,000; non-maintenance charge of Rs 1,000 per month.
- Cash deposits free up to Rs 2 lakh per month; Rs 4 per Rs 1,000 thereafter.

## Fund transfers
- **UPI and IMPS** (up to Rs 5 lakh): free.
- **NEFT and RTGS** through the app or internet banking: free. At the branch: NEFT Rs 2 to Rs 25 depending on amount, RTGS Rs 20 to Rs 45.
- International outward remittance: Rs 500 per transaction plus correspondent bank charges.

## Debit card
- Annual fee: Rs 250 (RuPay Classic) or Rs 500 (Visa Platinum); waived if AMB is maintained.
- Replacement card: Rs 200.
- ATM withdrawals: 5 free per month at SmartBank ATMs; 3 free at other banks' ATMs in metro cities (5 in non-metro). Beyond the free limit: Rs 23 per financial and Rs 10 per non-financial transaction.
- International usage: 3.5% foreign currency markup on transactions abroad or with foreign merchants.

## Credit card
- Joining and annual fee depend on the card variant; the annual fee is waived on spends of Rs 1.5 lakh per year.
- Finance charge on revolving balance: 3.5% per month (42% per year).
- Late payment fee: Rs 100 (balance up to Rs 500) to Rs 1,300 (balance above Rs 50,000).
- Cash advance fee: 2.5% of the amount, minimum Rs 500.
- Foreign currency markup: 3.5%.

## Other charges
- Duplicate statement: Rs 100 per statement (free in the app).
- Stop payment of cheque: Rs 100 per instruction.
- NACH / auto-debit return: Rs 500 per instance.
- SMS alerts: free for transaction and security alerts.
