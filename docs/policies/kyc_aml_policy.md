# SmartBank KYC and Anti-Money Laundering Policy

*Fictional document created for the SmartBank AI demo. Version 2.0, effective 1 January 2026.*

## Why KYC is required
Know Your Customer (KYC) verification confirms the identity and address of every customer. It protects customers from identity theft and helps the bank prevent money laundering and terrorist financing. Accounts with pending KYC operate with restrictions until verification is completed.

## Accepted documents
**Proof of identity** (any one): Aadhaar card, passport, voter ID card, driving licence, or NREGA job card.
**PAN**: the Permanent Account Number card (or Form 60 if the customer has no PAN) is mandatory for all accounts.
**Proof of address** (any one, if not on the identity document): Aadhaar, passport, utility bill not older than 2 months, registered rent agreement, or bank statement with the current address.
**Photograph**: a recent photograph captured during video KYC or at the branch.

## Ways to complete KYC
1. **Video KYC** in the SmartBank app (8 AM to 8 PM, all days): a live video call with a bank officer; keep the original PAN card and a blank white paper and pen ready. Takes about 10 minutes.
2. **Aadhaar e-KYC with OTP**: quick but limited. Accounts opened this way have a balance cap of Rs 1,00,000 and must be upgraded to full KYC within 12 months.
3. **Branch visit** with original documents.

Verification is normally completed within 24 hours of a successful video KYC call.

## Restrictions while KYC is pending
- Maximum account balance: Rs 1,00,000.
- Total credits in a financial year: up to Rs 2,00,000.
- Loans, credit cards and international transactions are not available.
- Cash withdrawals are limited to Rs 10,000 per day.

## Periodic re-KYC
KYC must be refreshed every 2 years for high-risk customers, every 8 years for medium-risk and every 10 years for low-risk customers. Customers receive reminders 60 days before the due date. If there is no change in details, a self-declaration through the app is sufficient.

## Anti-money laundering monitoring
SmartBank monitors transactions for unusual patterns, including large cash deposits, rapid movement of funds, many small transactions that avoid reporting thresholds (structuring), and transfers to high-risk jurisdictions. Cash transactions of Rs 10 lakh or more in a month and all suspicious transactions are reported to the Financial Intelligence Unit as required by law. Customers may be asked to explain the source of funds for unusual transactions.

## Customer data protection
Bank staff and the SmartBank Assistant will never ask for your PIN, OTP, CVV or password. KYC documents are encrypted at rest and accessed only by authorised verification staff.
