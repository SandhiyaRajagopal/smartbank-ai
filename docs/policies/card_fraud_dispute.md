# SmartBank Card Security, Fraud and Dispute Policy

*Fictional document created for the SmartBank AI demo. Version 4.1, effective 1 March 2026.*

## Real-time fraud monitoring
Every card and UPI transaction is scored in real time by SmartBank's fraud rule engine on a 0-100 scale. The score considers how the amount compares with the customer's usual spending, whether the transaction is international without a travel notice, unusual hours (midnight to 5 AM), high-risk merchant categories such as gift cards, crypto exchanges and gambling, and the number of transactions in a short time window.
- **HIGH risk (60 and above)**: the transaction is flagged and the customer receives an instant critical alert in the app.
- **MEDIUM risk (30-59)**: the customer may be asked to verify the transaction.
- **LOW risk (below 30)**: processed normally.

## What to do if you see an unauthorised transaction
1. **Block your card immediately** using the SmartBank app or by asking the SmartBank Assistant. Blocking requires your confirmation in the app and takes effect instantly; all further card transactions are declined.
2. **Report the transaction** in the app under Disputes, or tell the SmartBank Assistant which transaction you do not recognise.
3. Do not share OTPs, PINs or CVVs with anyone, including callers who claim to be from the bank.

## Customer liability for unauthorised transactions
- **Zero liability**: if the fraud is due to a deficiency on the bank's side, or a third-party breach that you report within **3 working days** of receiving the alert.
- **Limited liability**: if reported within 4 to 7 working days, your maximum liability is Rs 10,000 for savings accounts and Rs 25,000 for current accounts and credit cards with limits above Rs 5 lakh.
- **Beyond 7 working days**: liability is decided as per the board-approved policy, after investigation.
- If the loss is due to customer negligence, such as sharing a PIN or OTP, the customer bears the full loss until the transaction is reported.

## Dispute resolution timelines
- The disputed amount is credited provisionally (shadow credit) within **10 working days** of reporting.
- Investigation is completed and the dispute is resolved within **90 days**.
- If you are not satisfied, you can escalate to the Grievance Redressal Officer and then to the Banking Ombudsman.

## Travel notices
Customers travelling abroad should set a travel notice in the app with destination and dates. With a travel notice, international transactions in those countries are not penalised by the fraud engine. International usage must be enabled on the card separately.

## Card replacement
A blocked card cannot be reactivated if it was blocked for suspected fraud. A replacement card with a new number is dispatched within 5-7 working days at no charge when the block is due to fraud. Recurring payments and standing instructions must be updated with the new card number.
