# SmartBank Account Terms and Service Standards

*Fictional document created for the SmartBank AI demo. Version 1.6, effective 1 January 2026.*

## Interest on savings accounts
Interest is calculated on the daily closing balance and credited quarterly (in April, July, October and January):
- Balance up to Rs 1 lakh: 3.00% per annum.
- Balance above Rs 1 lakh up to Rs 10 lakh: 3.50% per annum.
- Balance above Rs 10 lakh: 4.00% per annum.
Tax is deducted at source on fixed deposits as per the Income Tax Act; savings interest up to Rs 10,000 per year is tax-exempt under section 80TTA.

## Fixed deposits
Fixed deposits are available from 7 days to 10 years starting at Rs 5,000. Senior citizens (60 years and above) receive an additional 0.50% per annum. Premature withdrawal attracts a penalty of 1% on the applicable rate.

## Transaction limits
- UPI: up to Rs 1 lakh per transaction and Rs 1 lakh per day (Rs 5 lakh for verified tax and hospital payments).
- IMPS: up to Rs 5 lakh per day.
- NEFT and RTGS through the app: up to Rs 10 lakh per day (RTGS minimum Rs 2 lakh).
- Debit card: ATM withdrawals up to Rs 50,000 per day; purchases up to Rs 2 lakh per day. Customers can lower these limits in the app.

## Statements and alerts
Monthly e-statements are emailed free of charge. SMS and in-app alerts are sent for every debit, every credit above Rs 1,000, every security event and every high-risk transaction.

## Nominee and dormant accounts
Customers are encouraged to register a nominee. An account with no customer-initiated transaction for 24 months is classified as inactive (dormant); it can be reactivated by submitting updated KYC. Unclaimed balances after 10 years are transferred to the depositor education fund but remain claimable at any time.

## Grievance redressal
1. Raise a complaint in the app, by email to care@smartbank.demo, or at any branch. Acknowledgement within 24 hours; resolution target of 7 working days.
2. If unresolved after 30 days, escalate to the Principal Nodal Officer.
3. If still unresolved, the customer may approach the Banking Ombudsman under the Integrated Ombudsman Scheme.

## Use of AI assistance
The SmartBank Assistant uses AI agents to answer questions and help with banking tasks. Decisions on fraud risk and loan eligibility are made by the bank's rule-based systems, not by the AI. Sensitive actions such as blocking a card or submitting a loan application always require the customer's explicit confirmation. Conversations are logged for audit and quality purposes, and personal identifiers typed in the chat are masked before processing.
